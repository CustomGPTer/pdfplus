What PDF+ does not do

PDF+ is an Android app only and is not on Google Play. Text editing works a line at a time, and PDF to Word or Excel carries over text and simple tables, not complex layouts. A digital ID you make yourself is not issued by a certificate authority, so other readers show it as unverified.
