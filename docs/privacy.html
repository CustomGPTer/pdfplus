Privacy policy

PDF+ is published by Jonathan Jackson. It is designed so that your documents stay on your device. There is no PDF+ account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your PDF files, the notes and signatures you add, the search index of the words in your documents, your reading positions, bookmarks, tags, settings, saved signatures and any digital ID are stored only on your device. PDF+ has no server and the publisher never receives any of this.

Permissions

  • All files access lets PDF+ list the PDFs on your device and save your changes back into them. It is used for nothing else.
  • Biometrics are used only if you turn on the app lock or open the private folder. The check is done by Android; PDF+ never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Internet is used only for the optional features below.

Optional features that use the internet

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, PDF+ asks Google for three permissions:

  • See and download your Google Drive files (read-only). PDF+ uses this only to list the folders you browse and the PDFs inside the folders you pick, and to download a PDF when you tap it. It cannot change or delete anything through this permission.
  • Its own hidden app-data folder, where PDF+ keeps one small file holding reading positions, bookmarks, favourites, tags and a few settings so they can follow you between your devices.
  • Files PDF+ itself created, used for the "PDF+ Sync" folder that mirrors your Synced folder.

This information travels directly between your device and your own Google account. It is never sent to the publisher or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or to train any model. PDF+'s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect in Settings at any time, and remove PDF+'s access in your Google account.

AI summaries and questions

This feature is off unless you turn it on and enter your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini. When you press a button to summarise or ask a question, the text of the document is sent from your device straight to the provider you chose, under your own account with them and their terms. Nothing is sent until you press the button. Your key is stored only on your device.

New-version check

About once a day PDF+ asks GitHub whether a newer release exists. This is an ordinary web request that includes your IP address, as any web request does. You can turn it off in Settings.

Scanning and text recognition

Scanning and OCR run on your device using Google's ML Kit and Google Play services. Your pages are not uploaded. Google may collect diagnostic and usage information about these components as described in Google's ML Kit terms.

Children

PDF+ is a general-purpose document tool. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact
