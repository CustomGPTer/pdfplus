Version 1.0 14 November 2026

First release.

  • Library with recent files, folders, favourites, tags, collections, a private folder and search inside documents.
  • Reader with scroll, single and two-page layouts, deep zoom, colour modes, outline, thumbnails, bookmarks, tabs, reading view and read aloud.
  • Full annotation set, form filling, drawn and certificate signatures, basic text and picture editing, and form field creation.
  • Page tools, drawing scale and measuring, layers, true redaction, passwords, app lock.
  • Scan to PDF, OCR, merge, split, compress, flatten, watermark, page numbers, and conversion to and from pictures, text, Word and Excel.
  • Optional Google Drive folders and sync, optional AI summaries with your own key, and a new-version check.

Every version is also listed with its download on the GitHub releases page.
